About

One prompt at a time, this space invites people to write honestly, listen to someone else, and respond with their voice.

How it works

You answer the current prompt anonymously.

You are then shown someone else's approved answer.

If you choose to leave a voice response, you read the prompt and their answer aloud first, then respond in your own words.
Anonymity

No public profiles, usernames, follower counts or likes.

Please do not include names, contact details, social handles or identifying information in your written or spoken response.
Moderation

Written answers and voice recordings may be reviewed before becoming available to others or appearing in the archive.
